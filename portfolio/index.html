Samuel Plattten

c75525.org

“c75525” is a blog created as an experiment in navigable, spatially plotted digital content. The site map, or page structure, is reflected in a dynamic diagram of a tube. Every post on the blog occupies an object displayed on one of its many concentric rings. Organic exploration of the blog’s contents is encouraged by dropping the user into a random post and giving them directional controls to browse further.

samuelplatten.com

An artistic portfolio website featuring a still, resting interface sporting a digital sculpture. Scattered along the structure are hyperlinks to various media works, exhibitions, DJ mixes, and social media channels.

saia.center

Social Anthropology Internet Archaeology (S.A.I.A.) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit located in Phoenix, AZ. Its mission is to educate the public on, and archive internet culture, while simultaneously stewarding art inspired by its research. The website serves as a portal to external channels.
